# BedWars – UEFN Verse Template

**Deutsch:** Ein BedWars-Spielmodus mit Betten, Team-Inseln, Ressourcen-Generatoren und PvP.

**English:** A bed wars game mode with beds, team islands, resource generators, and PvP.

## Features

- Team-Inseln mit Bett (Leben = Bett intakt) | Team islands with bed (life = bed intact)
- Ressourcen-Generatoren (Eisen, Gold, Diamant) | Resource generators (iron, gold, diamond)
- Brücken-Bau zwischen Inseln | Bridge building between islands
- Waffen- und Rüstungs-Upgrades | Weapon and armor upgrades
- Letztes-Team-Steg-Bedingung | Last-team-standing win condition

## Tech Stack

- UEFN, Verse, Creative Devices, TeamDevice

## Extension Ideas

- Emeralds für Spezial-Items | Emeralds for special items
- Team-Upgrade-Shop | Team upgrade shop
- Zuschauer-Modus nach Elimination | Spectator mode after elimination
