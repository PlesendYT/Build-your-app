Artificial intelligence (AI) is the simulation of human intelligence in machines that are programmed to think and learn. Machine learning is a subset of AI that enables systems to learn from data without explicit programming. Deep learning uses neural networks with many layers to model complex patterns.

Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) combines information retrieval with text generation. It first retrieves relevant documents from a knowledge base, then passes them as context to a language model for answer generation. This approach improves factual accuracy and reduces hallucination.
